# Superstore Sales Analysis using SQL

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a Superstore sales dataset using SQL. The goal is to answer real-world business questions related to sales performance, customer behavior, regional performance, and product trends.

This is my first SQL Data Analysis project, created to practice SQL concepts and gain hands-on experience with data analysis.

# Business Questions Solved

## Level 1: Exploring the Data

1. How many unique customers are there?
2. How many cities does the company serve?
3. What are the distinct product categories?
4. What are the distinct shipping modes?

---

## Level 2: Sales Analysis

5. What is the total sales revenue?
6. Which category generates the highest sales?
7. Which sub-category generates the highest sales?
8. Which region generates the highest sales?

---

## Level 3: Customer Analysis

9. Who are the top 10 customers by total sales?
10. Which customer placed the most orders?
11. How many customers belong to each segment?
12. What is the average sales per segment?

---

## Level 4: Geographic Analysis

13. What are the top 10 cities by sales?
14. What are the top 10 states by sales?
15. Which city has the highest number of orders?
16. Which region has the highest number of unique customers?
17. Which state has the highest average sales?

---

## Level 5: Product Analysis

18. What are the top 10 products by sales?
19. Which category has the most orders?
20. Which sub-category has the most orders?
21. What are the top 5 sub-categories by number of orders?
22. Which products were ordered more than 10 times?
23. What percentage of total sales does each category contribute?
